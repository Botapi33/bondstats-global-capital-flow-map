# Data Sources & Rights

## World Bank Open Data
Indicator: **Portfolio Investment, net (BoP, current US$)** (`BN.KLT.PTXL.CD`).

Source page: https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BN.KLT.PTXL.CD
License shown by World Bank: **CC BY 4.0**.

BondStats uses the numerical observations, provides source attribution, and transforms them into an original interface and normalized analytical pressure score. The underlying indicator's source metadata may identify IMF Balance of Payments Statistics; this tool obtains the data through the World Bank open-data distribution and its displayed license.

## Not represented
The animated routes are **not** observed bilateral transfers. They visualize directional pressure between negative and positive net-flow zones. Market-session status is clock-based context and is not a volume measurement.
